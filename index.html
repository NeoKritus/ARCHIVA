HOW TO USE

FILING YOUR RECORD
  1. ENTER YOUR STUDENT NAME AND ROLL NUMBER.
  2. YOUR ACADEMIC DETAILS ARE KEPT READY FOR THE CALCULATION.
  3. SELECT ONE OR MORE SEMESTER SHEETS — ANY COMBINATION, IN ANY ORDER.
  4. MARK THE GRADE FOR EVERY SUBJECT IN THE ACTIVE SHEET.
  5. SELECT CALCULATE & SEAL.
  6. YOUR CGPA IS STAMPED IMMEDIATELY.
  7. SELECT VIEW COMPLETE ACADEMIC REPORT FOR THE FULL SUBJECT-WISE BREAKDOWN.
  8. USE DOWNLOAD OR SHARE TO GET A PDF OR SHARE YOUR RESULT.
VALIDATION

IF REQUIRED INFORMATION IS MISSING OR INCORRECT, A SMALL MESSAGE APPEARS BELOW THE RELEVANT FIELD. AFTER YOU CORRECT THAT FIELD, THE MESSAGE DISAPPEARS.

RESET

CLEARS THE CURRENT STUDENT DETAILS, SEMESTER SELECTIONS, GRADES AND RESULT AFTER CONFIRMATION.

THEME

SWITCH BETWEEN LIGHT MODE AND DARK MODE AT ANY TIME.

ABOUT

ARCHIVA

RECORD YOUR GRADES. TRACK YOUR PROGRESS. KNOW YOUR CGPA.

PURPOSE

ARCHIVA CALCULATES SEMESTER GPA AND CGPA USING THE GRADING FORMULA.

FEATURES

SEMESTER-WISE GRADE ENTRY, AN INSTANT SEALED CGPA, A DETAILED ACADEMIC REPORT, ARREAR TRACKING, AND DOWNLOADABLE OR SHAREABLE PDF REPORTS WITH WATERMARK AND PAGE NUMBERING.

DATA & PRIVACY

ALL CALCULATIONS HAPPEN LOCALLY IN YOUR BROWSER. YOUR DETAILS ARE SAVED ONLY ON THIS DEVICE TO RECOVER AN UNFINISHED SESSION, AND ARE NEVER SENT TO A SERVER.
